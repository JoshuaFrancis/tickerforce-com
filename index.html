Platform overview

Three products that keep traders ahead of the market.

01

Breaking market news

A curated news feed delivering earnings reports, analyst upgrades and downgrades, merger and purchase announcements, regulatory filings, and macroeconomic data releases within seconds of publication. AI-assisted summarisation extracts the price-relevant signal from each story and tags it by ticker, sector, and news category, letting traders filter by holdings rather than reading every headline as it lands.

02

Options flow and unusual activity

Real-time options order flow showing large block trades, unusual volume spikes relative to open interest, and sweep orders executing across multiple exchanges simultaneously. A daily heat map surfaces the tickers with the most significant unusual options activity, allowing traders to identify potential catalyst plays before the underlying stock moves and before the activity becomes widely reported in financial media.

03

Alert infrastructure

A customisable alert engine delivering push notifications, SMS, and email alerts when user-defined conditions are met: price thresholds, volume spikes, options flow events, earnings call start times, and news mentions. Alerts can be routed to Discord servers, Slack workspaces, and Telegram channels, allowing traders to share signal streams with their communities or embed TickerForce intelligence directly into their existing trading workflows.
